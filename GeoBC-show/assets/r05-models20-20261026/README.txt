Binary PLY stores original reference surface XYZ [mm], displacement U [mm], projected nodal von Mises [MPa], original zero-based node index, and surface triangles. Original TET10 boundary faces are split into four triangles. These surface exports and quantized web files are for visualization, not replacements for full-volume FE training labels. Display stress is volume-weighted nodal averaging of element four-GP maximum VM; raw GP peaks are separately reported. Original FE material, field files and dataset splits remain unchanged. Equilibrium is not stability.
